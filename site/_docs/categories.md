# Built-In Category Templates

[&larr; Back to Overview]({{ '/' | relative_url }})

Wherezy ships **27 starter templates** (plus a **Blank Category** option) tailored for everyday households, tools, technology, and regional / festival storage. Every template can be customized or extended using the drag-and-drop Form Builder.

## Festival, Spiritual & Seasonal

- **Festival & Celebration Supplies** — Diwali diyas, string lights, torans, rangoli colors, Eid/Christmas/Holi decor, and reusable festival boxes
- **Puja Mandir & Samagri** — Brass idols, thalis, agarbatti, camphor, ghee wicks, sacred texts, and auspicious storage notes
- **Sarees, Ethnic & Heritage Wear** — Silk/cotton weave, zari type, blouse pairing, starch/dry-clean care, and cedar/mothball protection
- **Jewelry, Silver & Heirlooms** — Metal purity, bill/hallmark ref, locker vs. home storage, set completeness
- **Monsoon & Seasonal Protection** — Raincoats, umbrellas, tarps, Silica gel packs, mosquito nets, and winter quilts

## Household, Kitchen & Utilities

- **Pantry & Dry Goods** — Rice/dal/spices/flours, weight/pack size, seal state, and expiry tracking
- **Kitchen & Cookware** — Pressure cookers, cast iron, mixers, induction compatibility, spare gaskets
- **Inverter, Appliances & Water Systems** — UPS batteries, RO purifier cartridges, stabilizer capacity, filter service intervals
- **Bedding & Linens** — Size, GSM/fabric, seasonal rotation bin
- **Cleaning & Laundry** — Detergents, disinfectants, microfiber kits, hazard notes

## Health, Documents & Essentials

- **Medicines** — Dosage, strength, batch/lot, prescription link, and expiry alerts
- **First Aid** — Bandages, antiseptics, thermometers, oximeters, expiry monitoring
- **Documents** — Passports, Aadhaar/PAN/deeds, property papers, renewal reminders
- **Keys & Locks** — Key tags, duplicate count, padlock combinations, vehicle fobs
- **Books** — Author, ISBN, lending status, shelf index
